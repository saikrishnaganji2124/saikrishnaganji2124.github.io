CURRENT ENTERPRISE PROJECT

AI-Powered Meeting Intelligence & Workflow Automation

An AI-powered platform that transforms meeting conversations into structured, trackable work. It identifies decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, and project context, then validates those insights before connecting them with real team workflows.

01CaptureMeeting transcript

Capture meeting conversations through transcripts, uploads, and live meeting workflows while preserving the context required for downstream analysis.

02UnderstandLLM analysis

Use cloud-powered LLM analysis to extract summaries, decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, and project context. Long transcripts are handled through multi-window processing.

03ValidateConfidence + context

Validate extracted actions using confidence scores, meeting context, duplicate detection, existing-ticket awareness, and cross-window consolidation to reduce incorrect or repeated work.

04ActJira / OpenProject

Connect validated actions with Jira and OpenProject, resolve assignees, identify duplicate or related work, and control ticket creation through confidence-based automation.

05RetrieveMeeting knowledge

Make previous meetings searchable through grounded retrieval, helping teams quickly find decisions, responsibilities, actions, and historical project context.

The engineering

Building dependable AI workflows

The platform combines structured LLM extraction, long-context processing, duplicate handling, confidence-based validation, and grounded retrieval to create reliable AI-driven workflows. Long meeting transcripts are processed while preserving important context across multiple analysis stages, helping the system identify meaningful actions without losing relationships between discussions.

Rather than treating every extracted action as final, the workflow evaluates confidence, context, existing work, and duplicate or related matches before allowing automation to continue. The goal is not simply to generate meeting notes, but to make AI-generated outcomes dependable enough to support real project workflows.

The system

Intelligence connected to execution

The system connects FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Next.js, cloud LLMs, Jira, OpenProject, Clerk, and retrieval into an end-to-end meeting intelligence workflow. Meeting conversations move from ingestion and AI analysis through validation and eventually into the project-management tools teams already use.

Grounded retrieval keeps previous meeting knowledge searchable, while Grafana, Loki, and LangFuse provide application monitoring and LLM tracing. This visibility helps evaluate model behavior, processing time, failures, and overall workflow reliability as the platform evolves.

Current engineering focus

Current engineering is focused on improving the reliability and efficiency of the complete meeting-to-action pipeline. Key areas include long-transcript processing, cross-window consolidation, duplicate-vs-related detection, existing-ticket awareness, confidence-based ticket gating, cloud-model evaluation, Jira/OpenProject integration, grounded retrieval, and AI observability.

The broader goal is to reduce incorrect or duplicate automation while improving how accurately meeting decisions become structured work.

Ultimately, the platform is designed to turn what teams discuss in meetings into accurate, validated, searchable, and actionable work—connecting conversation directly with execution.
